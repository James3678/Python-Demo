Mappings

X12 837 → FHIR Claim (use=claim), with Patient and Organization resources.
X12 278 → FHIR Claim (use=preauthorization), the PAS-style resource.
HL7 v2 ADT → Patient + Encounter, mapping PID to Patient and PV1 to Encounter.

Patterns in the code

Anti-corruption boundary: parse → map → emit, so X12 and HL7 structures never leak downstream.
Idempotency: deterministic UUIDs plus conditional-update transaction Bundles (PUT Patient?identifier=…) make replays safe.
Dead-letter handling: translate() routes failures to a DLQ list, which would be a Kafka topic in production.

Not production-ready

Only the main loops are handled. The 837 and 278 HL hierarchies (dependents, multiple service lines, 2300/2400 loop context) need full handling.
No code-set or terminology validation, and no FHIR profile validation against the Da Vinci PAS and CRD IGs.
A real build would use pyx12 or Edifecs for X12, hl7apy for v2, and HAPI or fhir.resources for validation.
